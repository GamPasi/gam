About US

Awareness Raising and Knowledge Building.

Youth Advocates conducts campaigns and educational programs to raise awareness on issues related to the health and well-being of young people, such as sexual and reproductive health, mental health, and education, with an end goal of breaking down stigmas and promote a more supportive environment for young people.

Our Services

    Youth Engagement

    We ensure inclusive reform and formulation of policies and legislation that provide the right framework for equitable use of SRHR services and access to opportunities.

    Economic & Social Empowerment

    YA innovative approach links integrated SRHR/GBV/HIV information and services with skill building and entrepreneurship for young people.

    Adolescents Health & Wellbeing

    We use science and human centred design to improve policies, programmes and deliver integrated SRHR services that enhance adolescents health and well-being.

    Youth Helpline

    A confidential contact centre offering free knowledge and skills towards adoption of protective sexual and reproductive health behaviors and Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse

    Let's Help Them Together

    Together we can advocate for change.

    Youth Advocates works with the government & communities to safeguard and promote the rights of children and young people. Over the years we have demonstrated experience with holistic programming for children, young people and women; integrating SRHR/HIV/GBV with livelihoods, climate action and social protection. We implement evidence-based programs that focus on impoverished areas in the community.
    We pioneer innovative grassroots programs that shift the focus from immediate behavioural factors to the structural drivers that directly and indirectly increase young people’s exposures to boot SRHR outcomes, HIV, poverty, gender inequality, sexual violence, public policies and lack of education.

    Together we can :

    • Advocate for our youngones.
    • Say no to poverty.
    • Promote gender equality.
    • Promote good health & well being in communities.
    • Advocate for zero hunger.
